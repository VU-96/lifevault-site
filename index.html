Your important documents. Private, offline, always on time.

LifeVault keeps passports, IDs, visas, licences and insurance for you and your family, and reminds you before they expire. Nothing leaves your phone.

Get it on Google Play Free. No ads. No account.

The permission list is the proof.

Any app can promise privacy. Android only lets an app do what it has asked permission for, and you can read LifeVault's list yourself in your phone's settings.

What LifeVault can do

  • Use the cameraOnly when you choose to scan a document.
  • Show notificationsTo remind you before something expires.
  • Use your fingerprint or faceOptional, to unlock without typing your PIN.
  • Start again after a restartSo reminders are still there when your phone reboots.
  • VibrateFor the small taps you feel when you press a key.

What it cannot do

  • Connect to the internetThe app does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot open a network connection.
  • Browse your photos or filesIt sees a file only when you pick that file yourself.
  • Record soundNo microphone permission.
  • Know where you areNo location permission.
  • Read your contactsNo contacts permission.

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting inside the app. We have no server for your documents to go to. How LifeVault protects your data

One calm place for the paperwork of a whole household.

Everything with an expiry date
Passports, national IDs, residence permits, visas, driving licences, insurance, vehicle registration, warranties, subscriptions, memberships and medical cards, plus anything else you want to add.
Reminders in good time
Choose when to be told: 90, 30 or 7 days before, on the day, or your own number. The notification never shows a document number or a name on your lock screen.
For the whole family
Add your partner, children and parents, and see whose document needs attention next.
Scan instead of typing
Photograph a document and keep the scan encrypted beside its details. A passport's machine-readable lines can fill in the form for you, and you confirm what was read before it is saved. The reading happens on your phone.
Locked
A six-digit PIN, optional fingerprint or face unlock, automatic locking when you leave the app, and screenshots blocked unless you allow them.
Your own backup
Save one encrypted file wherever you like, protected by a password only you know, and restore from it on a new phone.
English and Arabic
Both languages, with a proper right-to-left layout in Arabic.

There is no password recovery.

If you forget your PIN and lose your backup password, your documents cannot be recovered. Not by us, and not by anyone else. That is the price of a vault only you can open. Make a backup regularly, and keep its password somewhere safe.
