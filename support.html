Support

Answers to the questions people ask most. LifeVault has no accounts, so there is nothing we can look up or reset for you; these steps are everything.

I forgot my PIN

There is no way to recover it, and we cannot unlock the app for you. If you have a backup file and remember its password, uninstall and reinstall LifeVault, set a new PIN, then restore from the backup in Settings, "Backup & restore". Without a backup, the information in the vault is lost.

A reminder was late or did not arrive

  1. Check that notifications are allowed for LifeVault in your phone's settings. The Reminders screen in the app tells you if they are off.
  2. Some phones stop apps in the background to save battery. In the app, open Settings, "Reminders", then "Battery settings", and allow LifeVault to run in the background.
  3. Open the app from time to time. Each time it starts, it puts all reminders back in place.

Reminders arrive around the time you chose, not at the exact minute. Android may hold one back for a while to save battery.

Moving to a new phone

  1. On the old phone: Settings, "Backup & restore", then create a backup and choose a password.
  2. Copy the backup file to the new phone.
  3. On the new phone: install LifeVault, set a PIN, then restore from the file with the same password.

The vault is not moved by Android's own phone-to-phone transfer. That is deliberate.

How often should I back up?

After you add or change something you would not want to type again. The app shows when you last made a backup and reminds you if it has been more than 30 days.

Deleting everything

Settings, then "Erase all data", wipes the vault from the phone. Uninstalling the app does the same. Backup files and PDFs you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

Can I use my phone's lock instead of a PIN?

Yes, on Android 11 and newer. In Settings, "Security", choose "Lock with", then "Phone screen lock". LifeVault then opens with your phone's PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint. Anyone who knows your phone's screen lock can open it too.

Fingerprint or face unlock is not offered

Fingerprint or face unlock goes with the LifeVault PIN. Add a fingerprint or face in your phone's own security settings first, then switch on "Unlock with biometrics" in Settings, "Security". LifeVault accepts only Android's strongest class of biometrics, so on some phones face unlock is not offered and the fingerprint is.

Biometric unlock turned itself off

That happens when a fingerprint or face is added to the phone, or after updating from LifeVault 1.0.0 (1). Open the app with your PIN and switch "Unlock with biometrics" on again.

The scan filled in something wrong

Reading text from a photo is not perfect. The app shows what it read before saving, so you can correct it. Good light and a flat document help.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at present. LifeVault is for Android phones running Android 8 or newer.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There are no ads and no purchases in the app.
